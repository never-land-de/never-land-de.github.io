ENTRY I // NOTES FROM THE FRONTIER

A world in retreat

PERSONAL NOTES
XBOX SERIES X // 2026

More than an open world, Red Dead Redemption 2 feels like a place remembered. Its landscapes invite long detours, while the small routines of camp, trail and town give weight to a story about people who have already begun to outlive their own age.

The pace is part of that effect. Riding without a destination, watching weather move across the plains or noticing a stranger at the roadside can matter as much as any mission. The camera becomes less a tool for recording progress than a way of preserving moments before they disappear.

This page is a personal field journal: selected photographs from the journey, gathered by landscape, people and place. The images remain close to their in-game appearance; their different colours and monochrome treatments are part of the record rather than something to be made uniform.

“We are living through the closing pages of the West — and every photograph already looks a little like a memory.”

Landscape / Journey

The country is never just scenery. It measures the gang’s movement from refuge to refuge — frozen beginnings, temporary homes, unfamiliar shores and the quiet promise of a life somewhere beyond the next ridge.

The story lives in faces: confidence turning into desperation, companionship tested by flight, and Arthur slowly learning to see his own choices more clearly. These plates hold moments rather than a complete chronology.

ENTRY IV // PLACES REMEMBERED

Places / Memory

Places acquire meaning through what happened there. A modern city can feel older than the wilderness; a camp becomes home for only a few weeks; a hillside carries the weight of an entire journey.
