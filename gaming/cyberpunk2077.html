01 // PERSONAL LOG

Personal Notes

I have always had a thing for open-world games and futuristic — even dystopian — environments. Back in 2004, when Half-Life 2 was released, the potential of that combination was already hard to miss. So when I finally got to explore Night City in 2022, I was thrilled.

The learning curve is steep: its many role-playing systems give you the freedom to take V down genuinely different paths and assemble radically different builds. Once it clicks, however, the city becomes a remarkable playground. The storytelling and localisation are exceptional, and CD Projekt Red raised the bar again with the Phantom Liberty expansion.

This page is my small Night City archive: my latest character configuration, selected original and edited captures, pixel-art experiments, and a set of Windows savegames placed at useful branching points.
