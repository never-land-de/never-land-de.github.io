# Sisters Gig Archive – Migration Batch

## 2026 Köln

Date: 5 October 2026
City: Köln
Country: Germany
Venue: E-Werk
"Support:"" Real Farmers

Review:
A quite lovely day in Cologne. The Sisters have had a couple of dates before this one and -- as usual -- managed to get in a few surprises along the way.

But first, confusion. There was another concert happening at the Palladium just across the street so we (and not only we) first managed to get into the wrong queue. And while standing in that wrong queue, we were wondering about the quite interesting mixture of types in the audience ...

Anyhow. Get in, have a few chats and show my daughter (her very first concert overall!) what can be seen on stage while it is prepared, with so many people involved.

[Include here: panicmaru.jpg in full container width]

The tour itself is apparently called "PANIC MARU", with this logo on the tour shirt. The word „maru“ (丸), from what I've read, roughly means "round, circle, wholesome". An AI tells me after research that the maru shown as a brush stroke strongly resembles an Ensō, a calligraphic circle. The colour red in combination with a maru also strongly references the "Hinomaru", better known as the sun disc seen on the Japanese flag.

First in on the stage, "Real Farmer". The forums and online communities speak loudly of their rude behaviour that evening, so any word here on them is wasted. Or, as my daughter put it: "It will get better."

And it did. Standing on the side where Kai played (2nd row), sound was actually very good! Andrew's voice was good in the mix, and Kai is pure energy on stage. Joined in 2023, you could tell Kai made quite some impact in how songs are differently nuanced. The set itself was the same as the night before in Frankfurt. Starting with "Ribbons" never is a bad idea, though, with the "Spacetime" intro from the 2001 gigs playing, this will forever be connected in my head with the opening song "Crash And Burn", like in Hasselt back in the day.

Songs overall were a good mixture of the different stages of the band including a good selection of new material. "When I'm On Fire" and "On The Beach" still are favourites of mine, as well as "Summer" (the nearly-to-be single of 1998), "Crash And Burn" and "More". Overall, strong set, strong performance, happy crowd.

In hindsight, some new songs of the Pearson and Dylan years are left out for some time now. And there are good one's. We can still wish for "Romeo Down" or "Show Me", right? ;-)
