TECH

:: machines, systems & memory ::

Technology is rarely just a tool here. Some machines become places in time: an iBook running Tiger, an old iPod still syncing its library, a Mac or PC kept alive long after its official expiry date. Briding into new areas that AI provide. TECH is where I collect those experiments — retro Apple hardware, old and new Windows, rebuilds, upgrades and the occasional impractical idea that works precisely because it should not. Not a museum, but a working archive of machines and systems worth keeping alive.
