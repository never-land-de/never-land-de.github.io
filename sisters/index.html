:: the sisters of mercy ::

This is my corner of Never Land dedicated to The Sisters Of Mercy: records and collectibles, gigs I have seen since M'era Luna 2000, and fragments collected along the way. It is not intended as a definitive band archive. It is a personal one — shaped by the releases I found, the concerts I attended and the things that somehow stayed with me over the years.
