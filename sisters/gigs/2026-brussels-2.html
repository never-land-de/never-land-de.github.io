2026: Brussels — Kobayashi Maru

12 October 2026
Ancienne Belgique · Brussels, Belgium
Support: Soft Palms

"I don't believe in the no-win scenario." - Captain James T. Kirk, Enterprise commander on NCC-1701 about the "Kobayashi Maru".

Turns out the Sisters were about to do something epic. Soft Palms again did an incredible job to put the audience in the right mood. Then, the Sisters opened with "Don't Drive On Ice". For the set, some classic from the Pearson era got an airing, such as "Summer", "Crash And Burn" and "We Are The Same, Susanne". "Flood II" was a great rendition, as well as "Here". But the huge surprise came with the encores: The first-ever airing of "Afterhours"! Beautifully played!

After the gig, we bumped into Chris Catalyst, being an all-around nice lad. Shared a beer and my gratitude. Pretty much stayed with the community at the O'Reilly's and got rewarded with the signature guitar picks! A great addition to the setlist I managed to snag.

"We Are The Same, Susanne" ... Hearing this song brought back a feeling of bliss that felt like an endless summer in 2003. I was two years in with my beloved Anja (what turned out to be the start of us and with two beautiful children) and already carried the sheer joy of past concerts and festivals in my heart. Listening to "We Are The Same, Susanne" sends me back to that state of mind, knowing that however f***ed up the world is, we still yield the power to create something beautiful if we really put our hearts and minds to it. Really love this tune. Thank you for playing this one, Kai, Ben Christo, Chris Catalyst and of course 😎

Oh, and: Why vaping with all the fog, I asked Chris? The flavour, obviously.

To sum up the weekend: "Kobayashi Maru" refers to a scenario of a seemingly unsolvable mission -- and the Sisters embarked and did it. Where people were expecting a copy-and-paste of night two, they bang out a previously unplayed song on top of an intense performance.

Looking back to a wonderful weekend meeting, chatting, laughing and rocking with so many groovy people from within the Sisters Community. It meant the world to me. A memory to cherish no matter how much water goes under the bridge. Thank you for this, Daniela, Oliver, Sabrina, Ingo, Alexa, Alexander, Simon, Andrew, Giovanna, Thomas, Bert, Peter, Trevor and Caroline, Don, Andreas, that young punk couple from Sheffield and of course to all those I failed to tag in this post. Thank you all for a weekend extraordinaire.
