2026: Brussels — Panic Maru

11 October 2026
Ancienne Belgique · Brussels, Belgium
Support: Soft Palms

After seeing the band in Köln - the first-ever gig of my daughter - and the good impressions of the gigs so far, anticipation for the gigs in Brussels even rose. Seeing the Sisters play the legendary stage of Ancienne Belgique further added to this. Plus, gigs like the ones in Belgium are always a good opportunity to meet with the community.

I thought Soft Palms as support were a great choice, much better than the disastrous performance of the support in Cologne. Julia Kugel (guitar, vocals) and Scott Montoya (drums) played a tight set. Go check them out!

The Sisters came to stage and opened with "Ribbons". As it turned out, "Dominion / Mother Russia" was back in the fold, replacing the dub version of "First And Last And Always". Quite a joyful performance of the band, they really gave it all on night one. With that said, setlist was pretty much the "standard one", leaving many fans to anticipate night two - and hoping for some surprises. As expected, the "standard" setlist for night one of "Panic Maru", but then, the band really performed that one! Quite playful, very tight. I really liked "Colours", where Eldritch kept repeating, "this is not relevant".

During the encores, a mother with her daughter came up. Since the crowd was quite "enthusiastic", the daughter made way to the front row, followed shortly by her mother with me making room for them in an attempt to shield them from the mayhem in my back. Hope this got me some karma points ;-)

After the gig, we got to hang out in "Le Coq", talking about the gig and hearing what Trevor Ristow already has in the works for the successor of his fabulous book "Waiting For Another War". All I can say is that he had very interesting stories to share with the people he talked to, including Tony James and Jovanka von Wilsdorf. I just hope he gets a missing crucial puzzle piece and gets hold of The Legendary Mister Whammy, Adam Pearson ...

And, I got an answer on the following night to one burning question: "Why does Chris Catalyst vape on stage when there is already all the fog?"
